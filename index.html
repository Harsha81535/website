02 / How I think

From evidence
to implications.

The objective is not analysis for its own sake. It is to make the question, trade-offs and next decision more explicit.

01. Frame the question

Define the business decision, who it affects and what a useful answer needs to resolve.

02. Test the evidence

Connect data, assumptions and scenarios; distinguish observed facts from modelled outcomes.

03. Translate the result

Explain the financial or commercial implications, limitations and what should be validated next.
