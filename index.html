Project 01 · Fraud economics

FraudLens India

Is there a commercially attractive opportunity in digital-payment fraud?

An independent research and commercial-diligence project examining the market context, potential economics and strategic case for solutions addressing digital-payment fraud in India.

The question I started with

What needs to be true for the opportunity to be commercially attractive–and which assumptions most influence the conclusion?

Project 02 · Identity & onboarding

Reducing Identity Fraud Without Losing Good Customers

An evidence-led analysis of digital onboarding, fraud controls and the economics of manual reviews, focused on balancing risk reduction with a smooth customer experience.

The question I started with

How can financial institutions stop more fraud without making onboarding harder for genuine customers?

Open the interactive research appView source on GitHub
Project 03 · Inventory & margin

FreshMargin

Protecting margin without compromising availability.

An independent business-analysis case study connecting demand signals, inventory risk, expiry exposure and financial decisions in fresh-produce retail.

The question I started with

Where can better inventory decisions reduce avoidable expiry exposure while maintaining product availability?

Open the interactive business analysis appView source on GitHub
Project 04 · NLP & customer support

AI Banking Support Analyzer

Turning customer messages into service intelligence.

An NLP-driven banking support analytics project exploring sentiment and intent classification, escalation-risk signals, sentiment patterns over time and interactive message testing. The work included data preprocessing and model-based text analysis.

The question I started with

How can customer-support text help teams identify emerging issues, understand customer sentiment and prioritise cases that may need attention?
